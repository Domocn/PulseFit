# PulseFit 30-Day OTF-Style Workout Calendar
> 60 templates: 30 Orange 60 (2G) + 30 Strength 50
> Pattern: OTF rotates Endurance → Strength → Power → ESP with benchmarks and signature events

## Weekly Pattern
- **Mon/Wed/Fri**: Orange 60 focus days (rotating E/S/P/ESP)
- **Tue/Thu/Sat**: Strength 50 focus days (Upper/Lower/Total rotation)
- **Sun**: Mixed (Power or Endurance Orange 60 + Total Body Strength 50)
