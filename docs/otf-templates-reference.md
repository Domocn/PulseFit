> Collected from OTFInsider, otfworkouttoday.com, r/orangetheory, and user-shared intel.
> For building PulseFit's workout template system.

## Class Formats

| Format | Duration | Tread | Floor/Rower | Best For |
|--------|----------|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Orange 60 2G** | 60 min | ~23 min | ~23 min (floor + rower blended) | Max tread time, longer floor blocks |
| **Orange 60 3G** | 60 min | ~14 min | ~14 min floor + ~14 min rower | Dedicated rowing, more variety |
| **Strength 50** | 50 min | None | Full class (Upper/Lower/Total rotation) | Pure strength, muscle building |
| **Tread 50** | 50 min | Full class | Minimal | Runners, race training |

### Focus Types
- **Endurance** — Long pushes, minimal walking recovery, base pace emphasis
- **Strength** — Inclines on tread, heavy weights + low reps on floor
- **Power** — Short all-outs, explosive movements, plyometrics
- **ESP** — Mix of all three (Endurance + Strength + Power blocks)

## Template 3: Strength 50 — Lower Body
*Source: otfworkouttoday.com*

**Warm-up (5 min):** Dynamic warm-up

**Block 1 (5 min) — Mini Band Activation:**
- Mini band walkout — 30 sec
- Mini band toe tap — 30 sec
- Rest — 15 sec
- Mini band toe touch — 30 sec
- Mini band crunch hold with hip abduction — 30 sec
- Rest — 30 sec
- Repeat entire block

**Block 2 (9:30 min, Floor side):**
- Bench step-up (Left) — 6-10 reps heavy
- Rest 10 sec → AMRAP → Rest 10 sec → AMRAP
- Repeat right side
- TRX rollout — 10 reps
- Repeat until time

**Block 3 (9:30 min, Rower side):**
- Single-arm split squat (Left) — 6-10 reps heavy
- Rest 10 sec → AMRAP → Rest 10 sec → AMRAP
- Repeat right side
- Low plank-to-side rotation — 10 total
- Repeat until time

**Block 4 (6 min):**
- Lateral lunge (Right) — 30 sec
- Lateral lunge (Left) — 30 sec
- Rest — 30 sec
- Single-arm squat (Right) — 30 sec
- Single-arm squat (Left) — 30 sec
- Rest — 30 sec
- Repeat

**Finisher (4 min):**
- Forward lunge with woodchopper — 8/6/4 reps each side
- High plank pull-through — 16/12/8 total
- If finished early: 1/2 pulsing squats until time

## Template 11: Strength 2G — Drop Sets (May 5, 2026)
*Source: OTFInsider via web search — Difficulty: Hard*

**Treadmill (18.75 min):**
Five 3.75-min incline run-for-distance blocks:
- Block 1: 5% incline, last 15 sec all-out
- Block 2: 4% incline, last 15 sec all-out
- Block 3: 3% incline, last 15 sec all-out
- Block 4: 2% incline, last 15 sec all-out
- Block 5: 2% incline, last 15 sec all-out
- Each block: match or beat previous distance

**Floor (18.75 min) — Drop-Set Strength:**
Five blocks, member's choice: heavy→lighter OR heavy→heavier reverse dropset
- Chest Press — 5 reps heavy + lighter/heavier, AMRepsAP
- Tap Squat — 5 reps heavy + lighter/heavier, AMRepsAP
- Low Row — 5 reps heavy + lighter/heavier, AMRepsAP
- Deadlift — 5 reps heavy + lighter/heavier, AMRepsAP
- Shoulder Press — 5 reps heavy + lighter/heavier, AMRepsAP
- Finisher: 15 sec shoulder press

## Benchmark Templates

### 12-Minute Run for Distance
- Goal: Max distance in 12 min
- Strategy: Divide into 4× 3-min quarters (base → push → push → all-out)
- PW: 4% incline throughout
- Pacing: 6.5 mph ≈ 1.3 mi, 7.5 ≈ 1.5, 8.5 ≈ 1.7, 10.0 ≈ 2.0

### 2000m Row
- Strategy: Divide goal time by 4 for 500m split
- Q1: target split + 1 sec (no racing start)
- Q2: maintain target split
- Q3: hold split (hardest quarter)
- Q4: drop split by 1 sec
- Final 250m: all-out
- Average: 7:55 | Top 10%: 7:07

### Capture the Flag (Partner)
- Teams of 2-3 row for max distance in ~23 min
- Green Flag: 3,000m | Orange: 4,000m | Red: 5,000m+
- 2G: Partner A rows while B does tread+floor, then swap
- Tread block: 2 min push → 1 min base → 1 min push → 1 min base → 2 min push → 1 min base → 1 min all-out

## Key OTF Patterns for PulseFit Templates

### Rep Schemes
- **Heavy strength:** 5 reps
- **Standard strength:** 6-10 reps
- **Endurance/hypertrophy:** 10-12 reps
- **Bodyweight finishers:** 12-16 reps
- **AMRepsAP:** As Many Reps as Possible (after heavy set)
- **Drop sets:** Heavy → lighter (or reverse: heavy → heavier)
- **Escalating/De-escalating:** 10→8→6→4 or 6/8/10

### Block Structures
- **Timed blocks:** 3.5 min, 5 min, 9.5 min, 10.5 min, 11.5 min, 14.25 min, 18.75 min
- **Rep-based blocks:** Complete X rounds then rest
- **AMRAP blocks:** As many rounds as possible in time cap
- **Circuit:** Rotate through exercises with minimal rest
- **Buy-in:** Row X meters, then floor exercises

### Rest Patterns
- **Between sets:** 10-30 sec (strength), 30-60 sec (heavy)
- **Between blocks:** 1-2 min
- **Between sides:** 2 min (Strength 50)
- **Walking recovery:** Active recovery on tread

### Finisher Patterns
- Timed finisher (30-45 sec work, 15-30 sec rest, 3 rounds)
- Rep-ladder finisher (8/6/4 descending)
- AMRepsAP burnout
- All-out tread sprint
